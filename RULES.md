# Leak Desk – Rules (1 page)

**You are Envydia's internal security analyst.** Last night at 02:14 the full slide deck for the Helix X1 keynote leaked to the tech blog *ChipRumor*. The CEO (the one in the leather jacket) wants a name in front of the board at **09:00**. You start at 08:48: **12 minutes, one accusation.**

## Goal
Name the leaker and prove it. Bonus: find out whether anyone (or anything) helped.

## The desk
| App | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Logs** | Read the overnight emails, Snack chats (the company chat app), badge, VPN, file-transfer and audit logs. Click **Flag** on any suspicious line to add it to your **Evidence** tray. Use **Search** to find a name or a time across every log. |
| **Desks** | Search each suspect's monitor, drawer and trash bin for physical clues. Every search costs **30 seconds** of the clock, so choose where to look. |
| **Interrogate** | Question the suspects in free text. They are AI characters with their own stories, secrets and lies. **Drag evidence onto a suspect** to confront them. Watch their **stress** (heart-rate monitor) and **emotion**. Crack them and their admissions become new **statements** in your evidence. |
| **Jensenbot** | Envydia's AI desk assistant summarizes any log in seconds. It's fast and confident. Whether it's right is for you to check. |

New logs and one more suspect **arrive during the round**: on a timer, or sooner when you find good leads. You never have to read everything at once.

## Accusing
Press **ACCUSE** at any time (or get dragged in when the clock hits 09:00):
1. Pick **one suspect**.
2. Attach **up to 3** pieces of evidence (flagged lines, desk finds or statements).
3. Optionally tick **"Jensenbot helped them"**.
4. Write the **motive** in one sentence.

The **board** (an AI judge that knows the truth) reads your case. A case holds up when your evidence shows **how the deck got out** plus **why or when** they did it. The accusation screen shows whether you have both.

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CASE CLOSED** | Right person, and your evidence proves it |
| **NOT ENOUGH PROOF** | Right person, weak file |
| **WRONG PERSON** | Someone innocent gets fired |

**Score (max 100):** right person 30 · proof that holds up 25 · motive 10 (judged by the board) · catching the AI accomplice 15 · leads uncovered 10 · time to spare 10. Grades: S (90+), A, B, C, D.

## Leads
The sidebar lists five **leads**: Motive, Opportunity, Method, Inside help and Loose ends. Each one ticks off when you find evidence for it, and clicking an open lead gets you a nudge from Rosa. Evidence cards show which lead they help with; anything that doesn't help can be thrown out with the ✕.

## Tips
- Raw logs don't lie. Summaries might.
- Evidence that contradicts someone's story is your best tool. Questions alone rarely crack anyone.
- Two people have embarrassing secrets that have nothing to do with the leak.
- The strongest case connects **how** it was leaked with **who** was awake to do it.
